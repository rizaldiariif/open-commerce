Catalog Requirement

Turn /products into a collection browsing page: category tiles at the top, optional promo panels, a filter/sort bar, and a quiet grid of product cards. The page should prioritize scanning, comparison, and quick movement into product detail.

Keep the route simple. ProductsIndex should load catalog data and compose CollectionHeader, CategoryTileRail, FilterSortBar, and ProductGrid.
